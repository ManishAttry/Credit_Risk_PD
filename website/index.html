About the Project

What is Probability of Default?

Credit Risk Modelling

PD models estimate the likelihood a borrower will default within a 12-month or lifetime horizon. Banks use this to decide loan approvals, set interest rates, and calculate capital reserves.

IFRS9 Compliance

Since 2018, IFRS9 mandates that banks provision for expected losses before they occur. This project maps PD scores directly to Stage 1/2/3 classifications and calculates the required ECL.

Business Impact

Accurate PD models reduce credit losses, optimise loan pricing, and enable early intervention on deteriorating loans. This project quantifies $793M in expected credit losses across 261K test loans.
